# Smith v0.7.2

Smith 0.7.2 lets a Gemini session continue after a turn that stopped in the
middle of tool calls, and after tool calls made on another provider.

## Upgrade notes

- **Session formats are unchanged.** Sessions written by 0.7.1 and 0.7.0
  resume in 0.7.2. Runtime events in stream-json stay at event schema 16, and
  the result envelope stays at schema 3.
- **Sessions stuck on Gemini resume again.** 0.7.1 noted that a session which
  failed with `Config: Gemini signed continuation is incomplete or out of
  order` could not continue on Gemini. In 0.7.2 it can: resume it and send a
  new message.

## Fixes

- **Gemini accepts tool calls it did not sign.** Gemini checks the signature
  of every tool-call step in the current turn. It also checks earlier turns
  that ended without an answer, for example a turn that failed or was
  interrupted mid-tools. A step with no Gemini signature now starts with
  Google's documented placeholder, `skip_thought_signature_validator`,
  instead of being refused. Without it, Gemini answered `Config: Gemini
  provider rejected the request`, and Smith refused the current-turn case
  itself. This covers tool calls continued from another provider and turns
  saved before 0.7.1 kept Gemini's search steps. Earlier turns that ended
  with an answer are sent as before, because Google notes the placeholder
  can lower answer quality. Checked live on `google/gemini-4-argon`: a copy
  of a session that 0.7.1 could not continue answered from its sub-agents'
  results.

## Under the hood

- Agent Runtime moves to v0.6.2 (`e38809e`), agent-runtime PR #47.
